Cedar Bakery

Cedar Bakery is a fictional neighbourhood bakery used only to test the TicketTech chat widget. We bake sourdough, date bread and cardamom buns every morning.

Opening hours

Saturday to Thursday we are open from 6:30 to 21:00. On Friday we open at 14:00 and close at 22:00.

Prices and orders

A sourdough loaf costs 18 SAR and a box of six cardamom buns costs 24 SAR. Custom birthday cakes need 48 hours' notice and start at 150 SAR.

Delivery

We deliver within 5 km of the bakery for 10 SAR. Delivery is free for orders over 100 SAR.
